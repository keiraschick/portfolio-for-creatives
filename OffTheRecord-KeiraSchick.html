off the record

Brand Identity | Social Campaign | Advertising | Collateral


2024

  • RATIONALE

    For many popular musicians, songwriting and composing is a form of storytelling—an artistic process inspired by different people, memories, influences, and emotions.
    But how did they get there?
    Off The Record invites popular, talented artists into small intimate venues, creating an environment where they can share their musical journeys with their fans.

  • The Logo.

    I decided to keep it loose and chaotic, to represent the raw and unfiltered essence of the concert. An additional touch is the little record lp used as the period.
    Some logo process work.

  • The Social Campaign.

    We promote digital media puzzles that users can find on our social media accounts or as hidden stickers around the hosting city. Players must guess the distorted songs.
    Some ideation work.
  • As you guess correctly you begin to collect pieces. These shapes drift around slowly on the home page. By adding a gamified element, the hope is to gain a sense of intrigue and hype.
  • Once you collect all pieces they drift to the center to form a record. This is when the artist gets revealed!! By collecting all the pieces you’ve entered yourself into the draw for tickets.

  • The Posters.

    Each poster encapsulates the vibes of the artist using patterns and colour. Since the main concept is an autobiographical concert built on connection and authenticity, I used their portraits with emphasize on the eyes to replicate that feeling.
