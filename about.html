  • I’m a high school art kid from Medicine Hat, turned award-winning graphic designer in Vancouver. I just recently graduated from the IDEA program at Capilano University with a Bachelor of Design in Visual Communications. Through my experience (like many others) I’ve grown a love for brand identities, print, packaging, art direction, and illustration. So if you have any design projects floating around, you can throw them my way.
    I like my work to make an impressionable statement—so when I design—it’s going to be bold, bright, and relatable. All design solutions already exist, the key is to discover them. That’s why I intentionally pull from fascinating human experiences to mold meaningful connections between my work, audience, and community, no matter how big or small.
  • When I’m not designing (which feels like never) I’m usually reading fantasy novels, strolling in the wilderness, mixing music, tending to my frog Gib, taking a fat nap on my (very) comfy green couch, or just figuring out what to actually do in my spare time. But I’m never too busy for new opportunities, partnerships, or a wee coffee chat, so don’t be afraid to reach out!
