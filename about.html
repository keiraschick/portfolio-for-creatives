  • Hey! I'm
    Hey, I’m Keira! I’m currently in my 4th year of the IDEA Bachelor of Design in Visual Communications program at Capilano University. I specialize in branding and graphic design, but I'm eager to take on any illustration or UX/UI that’s thrown my way. Come on, throw it my way!
    My curiosity and storytelling is something very important to me and I try to uphold that mindset for every creative project. Imagination is the just the beginning of creating something wonderful. As a designer, I have the ability to visualize intangible concepts and intentionally form them into reality. My goal is to shape the world and mold meaningful connections for not only my clients and their audiences, but my friends, family and my community no matter how big or small. Every story matters.
    When I’m not designing (which feels like never) you can find me lost in a book, off in the wilderness, or taking a nap on my comfy green couch. But I’m never too busy for new opportunities or partnerships. Feel free to reach out and let’s talk business!
