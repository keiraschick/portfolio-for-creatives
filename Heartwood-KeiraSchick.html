  • What's the Story?

    Getting consumed in the hustle and bustle of life is overwhelming. But what if we could provide you a haven filled with solace. A place completely separated from the confines of everyday life. A lush forest ambience and backdrop that captivates and charms. Our desire is to harmonize the connection between humans and Mother Nature. To dine with Heartwood, is a grounding experience—from the immersive atmosphere—to the local food that awaits on your plate. An elevated culinary experience that lets you escape your ordinary life and nurture your presence with a meal infused with the soul essence of Vancouver’s land and tide.
