  • what is it?

    In Morpho we focus on the art and beauty of the tattoos rather than the person they’re on. Morpho is named after the Morpho butterfly (featured on the back cover) known for going through a physical change. Their blue wings enhance their beauty, just like tattoos would to a human. This magazine features the history and evolution of tattoos, the artist’s passion and relationship to the craft of ink on skin, and the external expression of self. Tattoos are more than just permanent ink, they’re an art form. A sense of recognition, respect, and culture.
